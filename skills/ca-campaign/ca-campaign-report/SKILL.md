# Report

**Purpose:** Five minutes of reading keeps the person in charge.

## Steps
1. Lead with what changed for users and maintainers.
2. Scorecard before and now; progress against timeframe, plainly.
3. All queued decisions together, each with a recommendation.
4. New or changed ADRs, one line each.
5. What was dropped as unnecessary.
6. Next milestone and its gates.
7. Replan if estimates are off by more than half.
8. List merges with their revert commands.

## Output
`docs/daneel/REPORT.md`, under one page.
