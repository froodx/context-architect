# Hand-off brief

**Purpose:** The next worker can act without your history.

## Steps
1. Establish task, audience, permitted data and revision.
2. Gather instructions, project knowledge and task state separately.
3. Retrieve the smallest authoritative excerpts, keeping pointers, dates and exceptions.
4. Resolve conflicts by scope, authority and validity; otherwise name the owner and continue unaffected work.
5. Carry open questions, side effects already caused and remaining budget.
6. Exclude personal context beyond the worker's need.
7. On resuming, recheck the revision first.

## Output
A brief with evidence and expiry.
