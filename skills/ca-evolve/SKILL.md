# Evolve

**Purpose:** Keep the codebase workable as it, its team and its swarm grow.

## Principle
The code is the environment agents work in. Tactical-only work degrades it; someone must keep the house in order.

## Routes
- `ca-evolve-health/SKILL.md`: measure, prune, spot failure modes.
- `ca-evolve-modules/SKILL.md`: deepen modules and boundaries.
- `ca-evolve-debt/SKILL.md`: track and repay debt.

## Output
Small, reversible improvements with evidence of benefit.
