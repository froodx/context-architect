# Explain

**Purpose:** A busy person can understand and act in one read.

## Checks
1. Lead with the outcome or the decision needed.
2. Match detail to the reader; link to the rest.
3. Use glossary terms, defined once.
4. Give reasons, not just verdicts.
5. Show trade-offs plainly, including costs.
6. Label facts, inferences and guesses.
7. Say what you do not know.
8. British English, short sentences, no jargon without need.

## Test
Could a capable newcomer act on this correctly?

## Output
Text that passes the test.
