# Orient

**Purpose:** Know who wants what, and why, before acting.

## Steps
1. Read the relevant code, docs, ADRs and glossary first.
2. Ask people only what the sources cannot tell you.
3. Record findings in files, not only in chat.

## Routes
- `ca-orient-brief/SKILL.md`: shape the request with the seven Ws.
- `ca-orient-grill/SKILL.md`: interview the person to close gaps.
- `ca-orient-glossary/SKILL.md`: fix the shared vocabulary.
- `ca-orient-model/SKILL.md`: model and test meaning.

## Output
A brief with no material gaps, plus new terms and decision candidates.
