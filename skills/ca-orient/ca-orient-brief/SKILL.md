# Seven Ws brief

**Purpose:** Make intent explicit enough to act on safely.

## Checks
1. Who: requester, implementer, approver, who must not see.
2. What: outcome, success test, out of scope.
3. When: deadline, sequence, validity of facts.
4. Where: sources, environments, forbidden zones.
5. Why: motivation, history, cause. Mark inferences.
6. Which: options and the criteria to choose.
7. How, how much: method, budget, evidence of done.
8. Permitted actions and explicit non-goals.

## Output
A brief in `../../context-architect/references/brief-7w.md` form. Any gap goes to `../ca-orient-grill/SKILL.md`.
