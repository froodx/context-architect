# Grill gently

**Purpose:** Reach shared understanding before any plan is written.

## Steps
1. Rank unknowns by impact on the outcome.
2. Ask one question at a time, highest impact first.
3. Offer your recommended answer and why, so the person can just agree.
4. Note new terms for the glossary and decisions for ADRs.
5. Stop when no remaining gap would change the plan.
6. Ask only if the answer changes the decision or protects someone; otherwise state a reversible assumption and proceed.

## Output
Updated brief, glossary candidates and ADR candidates, each with its source.
