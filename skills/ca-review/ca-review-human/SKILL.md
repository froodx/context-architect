# Walk the human journey

**Purpose:** See the change as the people living with it will.

## Steps
1. Pick an affected person and task, including operators and support staff.
2. Follow ordinary use, interruption and recovery.
3. Inspect what they actually see or hear.
4. For consequential submissions: can they undo, correct or review before committing?
5. Consider accessibility and the cost of confusion.
6. Separate observation from assumptions about users.
7. Values in conflict? Name the human decision owner.

## Output
The consequence in plain words, one acceptance example and a recovery action.
