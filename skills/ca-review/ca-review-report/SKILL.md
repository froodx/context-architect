# Review report

**Purpose:** Easy to act on, honest about its limits.

## Steps
1. Verdict: approve, revise, needs a human decision, or insufficient evidence.
2. Say why in plain language.
3. Label comments: issue, suggestion, question, nitpick, praise; blocking or not.
4. Unsure? Use question, not issue.
5. Verify each finding; give location, evidence, impact and fix.
6. Mark regressions apart from existing debt.
7. List ADR and ratchet candidates.
8. State confidence and unreviewed paths.

## Output
A report in `../../context-architect/references/review-report.md` form.
